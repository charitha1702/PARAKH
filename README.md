# PARAKH — Don't Just Trust. Verify.

> **Before you believe it. PARAKH it.**

PARAKH is an AI-powered financial content verification platform built for Bharat.
It helps users investigate suspicious financial messages, screenshots, websites, claims, and voice inputs by detecting risk signals, tracing relationships, checking available evidence, and clearly explaining what can — and cannot — be verified.
PARAKH is not designed to tell users what to buy, sell, or hold.

It helps them understand **what deserves verification before they act.**

---
##  The Problem

Financial information now reaches people through:
- WhatsApp and Telegram
- Social media
- Screenshots and forwarded messages
- Websites and online communities
- Unsolicited calls and voice messages

A message can look official.  
A screenshot can look convincing.  
A website can look legitimate.

But users often don't have enough context to answer:

> **"Why should I trust this?"**
Existing solutions often stop at:

> **"This looks suspicious."**
PARAKH goes further.

It asks:
> **"Can the claim actually be verified?"**

---
##  Our Solution
PARAKH acts as a **verification layer between financial information and financial action.**

Users can submit:
- 💬 Messages
- 🖼️ Screenshots
- 🌐 Websites and links
- 🎙️ Voice inputs

PARAKH then:
**UNDERSTANDS → DETECTS → TRACES → VERIFIES → EXPLAINS → CHALLENGES**

Instead of producing an opaque "93% scam" score, PARAKH separates:
- What AI detected
- What evidence supports the claim
- What contradicts it
- What the community reports
- What could not be independently verified

#  Key Features

##  1. Multimodal Financial Content Analysis

Analyze suspicious:
- Messages
- Screenshots
- Websites
- Links
- Voice inputs

PARAKH identifies suspicious claims and behavioral patterns within the submitted content.

---
##  2. Scam DNA

### Understand the pattern, not just the warning.

PARAKH identifies behavioral signals such as:

- Urgency and artificial deadlines
- Guaranteed-return claims
- Authority impersonation
- Payment pressure
- Phishing indicators
- Manipulative financial language
- Suspicious requests for sensitive information

**Scam DNA is a behavioral fingerprint — not a fake probability of fraud.**

---
##  3. Trust Chain Reconstruction

### Where does trust break?

PARAKH reconstructs the relationships behind a financial claim.

```text
CLAIM
  ↓
ORGANIZATION
  ↓
WEBSITE
  ↓
CONTACT
  ↓
PAYMENT ROUTE
  ↓
EVIDENCE
```
The system can identify relationships that are:

- Verified
- Supported
- Unverified
- Contradictory
- Missing evidence

The goal is not simply to flag an entity.

It is to show **where the available evidence stops supporting the claim.**

---
##  4. "Show Me How You Know"

PARAKH makes explainability part of the product.

Users can inspect an important conclusion through an evidence trail:

```text
CLAIM
  ↓
SOURCE
  ↓
CHECK
  ↓
RESULT
  ↓
EXPLANATION
```
---
##  Evidence Transparency

Evidence is clearly separated into:

| Evidence Type                  | Meaning                                   |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| **Official Evidence**          | Evidence from authoritative sources       |
| **Reliable External Evidence** | Supporting evidence from credible sources |
| **Community Evidence**         | User-submitted experiences and reports    |
| **AI Analysis**                | AI-generated interpretation               |
| **Unverified**                 | Insufficient evidence available           |

> **AI inference is never presented as official evidence.**

---
##  5. Challenge PARAKH

### Don't blindly trust PARAKH either.
Users can challenge an analysis and ask the system to look for:

* Contradictory evidence
* Alternative explanations
* Supporting evidence
* Missing context
Possible outcomes include:

* **Contradictory evidence found**
* **No reliable contradiction found**
* **Evidence remains inconclusive**

PARAKH is designed to communicate uncertainty rather than hide it.

---

## 6. Community Intelligence

### One report is a warning. A pattern is a signal.

Users can report suspicious experiences and attach supporting evidence.

PARAKH can identify recurring signals across reports, such as:

* Repeated websites
* Phone numbers
* Payment routes
* Message templates
* Impersonated organizations
* Similar scam tactics

This enables detection of **possible emerging scam patterns**.

> Community reports provide context. They do not automatically prove fraud.

##  10. Safety by Design

PARAKH is designed to protect users without becoming another source of risk.

### PARAKH does **not** request:

* OTPs
* UPI PINs
* Passwords
* Bank credentials
* Card details
* Unnecessary sensitive financial information

### PARAKH does **not** provide:

* Buy recommendations
* Sell recommendations
* Hold recommendations
* Stock price predictions

Its purpose is:

> **Pause. Verify. Understand. Then act.**
